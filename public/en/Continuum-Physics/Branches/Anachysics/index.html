Overview

Anachysics is the formal study of the structures, interactions, and governing principles of souls and Sources within the Continnuum. It focuses on the behavior of the anima field, its coherence, and its coupling with the mana field through trans-phase resonance.

Anachysics specializes in the dynamics of living coherence, namely the feedback processes that sustain the self and permit interaction between thought and metaphysical energy. It connects the informational domain of animaspace with the energetic domain of manaspace, forming the theoretical foundation for life, consciousness, and transcendence within the Continuum.

The Laws of Anachysics describe how Continuum invariants manifest in the coupling between anima and mana. They define the limits of stability and evolution of coherent beings, such as how souls maintain identity, how Sources mediate energy across phase boundaries, and how both may fail and adapt.

In essence, anachysics is the physics of existence through awareness and the study of how the Continuum sustains selfhood as a stable field phenomenon.
